† Sources, definitions and thresholds

Sources. RingCentral call log (273 records for the Arizona day, pulled live via API), AgencyZoom API (leads, quotes, policies, tasks, service tickets), Insightful daily report email, Coach AI daily call and role-play emails, AgencyZoom daily recap email. Every figure is computed at build time; nothing is carried over from a previous report.

Arizona time. The reporting day runs 00:00–24:00 Arizona, UTC-7, no DST ever. AgencyZoom notes are timestamped Arizona-local while lead, policy and task records are UTC; both conventions are handled explicitly.

Call Volume counts distinct numbers dialed, not raw dials, and covers new business only. Service and renewal contacts are excluded entirely, per Frank’s definition. Classification excludes a number when its household has a service or renewal task due that day assigned to the same producer, a service or renewal ticket opened that day with that producer as CSR, or no lead record at all. On this day only Crystal had renewal work: her 69 distinct numbers resolve to 56 new business plus 4 renewal, 2 service and 1 matching no AgencyZoom record.

Team Call Volume is the sum of the per-producer figures (56 + 42 + 50 = 148). The union of distinct numbers across the team is 157 before new-business filtering, because one number was dialed by two different producers; the per-producer sum is used so that the team panel and the individual panels agree.

Live Contact is not a duration rule. It is established from producer-written AgencyZoom notes cross-referenced to the call log — a 28-second call can be a live contact while a longer one is a voicemail. A short call can be a live contact while a longer one is a voicemail; only the recording settles it. Of the 13 live contacts on this day, 8 rest on producer-written evidence and 2 have no written outcome and rest on call duration alone.

Avg Talk Time is total talk across live contacts divided by the live-contact count, not across all dials.

Premium Quoted combines stage-tracked quotes and AgencyZoom’s quoted-premium field, since a rep can quote without moving the pipeline stage. Quotes carry no creation date of their own, so they are dated from the lead’s quote date where present and otherwise from the stage-move note into a quoted stage.

Recontact stage groups — retained for future use. The three-stage view is not rendered in the report at present, but the definitions remain live in the build and are recorded here so nothing is lost. Stage names are matched across every pipeline, since the same stage name in a different pipeline serves the same purpose.
1. Contacted, In Progress → Stall — agreed to a quote, never followed through; sat 5+ days before going cold. Matches stage names containing contacted or in progress.
2. Ready to Present → Quoted — quote is done; how long and how many calls to get them back on the phone to go over it. Matches ready to present.
3. Quoted → FSD (Pending Bind) → Sold — client verbally agreed, waiting on their payday to actually bind. Matches quoted, quotes presented, FSD or pending bind.
A caveat that still applies: on the days measured, almost every lead in a Ready to Present stage arrived there by bulk move or automation rather than from a phone conversation, so group 2 reads near-empty. That is a process finding, not a metric fault.

Roster and team scaling. Team sum metrics scale ×3, for the 3 producers whose numbers are being counted. Coral Barwick and Sarahi Chin are excluded from every calculation on this report and appear as placeholder cards only: they are in training, their assigned leads are training data rather than book, and their dials are onboarding activity. Once they are included the team goals become ×5 — Call Volume 250+ green / 200–249 yellow, Households Quoted 25+ green / 10–24 yellow — and team panels should be expected to read red while they ramp. That decision is scheduled for review on 2026-08-28.

Thresholds. Rate, percentage and per-unit metrics apply the per-producer threshold unscaled — a rate does not change with headcount. Straight-sum metrics scale ×3. Boundary rules: premium red is under $501; task completion green is exactly 100% and yellow is 90% up to but excluding 100%; contact rate at exactly 13.0% is green; premium sold with zero policies is red rather than blank.

Goal status is shown as colored text on the value itself. The legend keys the three tiers.

Scope. Tasks whose AgencyZoom due date is Aug 12, 2026 and whose assignee is one of the 3 producers dialing that day: 167 tasks, 154 marked complete, 13 cancelled, 0 left open. Agency-wide the same day had 226 due and 200 complete (88.5%), the difference being Amanda’s 30 tasks with 19 complete plus small counts for Frank, Francisco, Debbie, Coral and Sarahi.

Note on the previous report’s figure. The earlier approved report stated 154 of 167 tasks (92.2%). That denominator could not be reproduced from the live API under any assignee scoping, date field, or status treatment tested — the closest principled definition landed at 169/154, and no subset of assignees yields either 167 due or 154 complete. The figures above use the definition stated in this section, which is reproducible and auditable. This is a deliberate departure from the previous report and worth Frank’s confirmation.

Matching a task to a call resolves every phone number on the linked lead or customer record and looks for an outbound RingCentral call from that rep to any of those numbers on the reporting day. A task whose linked record has no resolvable number is reported as unverifiable rather than counted either way.

Live contacts in the Call Detail table are established from producer-written AgencyZoom notes matched to the call log by number and duration to the second, not from a duration threshold.
