## 1. Speed to Dial — the whole card is wrong  [SETTLED]

Two lines in `build_day.py` (40-43) build the Team card from the **list of
per-producer medians**, never from the underlying lead times:

```python
meds = [v["median"] for v in s2d.values() if v]
team_s2d = {"median": sorted(meds)[len(meds)//2],
            "quickest": min(meds), "longest": max(meds)}
```

What went out tonight vs the truth:

| | Shipped | True |
|---|---|---|
| Team median | 1m 58s | **22s** |
| Team quickest | 11s | **9s** |
| Team longest | 1m 58s | **20m 47s** |

`longest = max(medians)` can never exceed the largest producer median, so it is
structurally guaranteed to read lower than the worst individual longest. This is
wrong every day, not just tonight. `quickest = min(medians)` is the same defect
mirrored. `median` is a median-of-medians and with `[len//2]` on an even count
takes the UPPER middle — with two qualifying producers it literally returns the
slower one's median.

Pooled true distribution, 17 leads:
`9, 11, 11, 11, 11, 16, 18, 19, 22, 24, 25, 28, 118, 135, 142, 1154, 1247`

**Fix, per Frank:**
1. Team quickest = fastest single dial by anyone.
2. Team longest = slowest single dial by anyone.
3. Team median = median of ALL dial times pooled.
4. Per-producer median to use the same true-median definition (average the two
   middle values on an even count). Currently `v[len(v)//2]` inflated Crystal to
   1m58s when her true median is 1m13s — and that median drives the colour tier,
   which cuts off at 2 minutes, so she was 2 seconds from a wrong colour.
5. Remove producers who received no internet leads. No footnote — Frank sets
   lead distribution and does not need it reported back.
6. If NOBODY received internet leads, remove the whole panel.

Requires `speed_to_dial` (daily.py 639) to stop discarding the raw per-lead
lists, since the team figure has nothing true to work from today.

Note for whoever implements: team extremes will now often equal some producer's
extremes (team longest 20m47s IS Lorena's). That is correct by definition and
should not be "fixed" later.

## 12. FSD and stage-error display design  [SETTLED on encoding; NEEDS FRANK on the stage map]

Frank: *"so how would we badge/display it as FSD, but also flag/badge the
incorrect stage"*

These are two semantically different things and must be encoded differently.

### FSD goes in the OUTCOME CHIP, not a second badge

A pending bind IS a call outcome, so it belongs where outcomes live. There is a
clean slot in the existing system:

```
Sold on the call        #008300  FILLED    bound now
FSD (Pending Bind)      #008300  STRIPED   bound later    <- NEW
```

The palette already uses filled-vs-striped WITHIN one hue to mean "this call"
vs "follow-up" — that is exactly how the blues and oranges work. Striped green
therefore reads as "sold, but not on this call", which is what a future sale
date is.

**This also sidesteps the HANDOFF_11 §7 blocker.** Nine categories on six hues
today; a tenth HUE needs the deferred colour-by-family rework. This is a tenth
CATEGORY on the existing six hues, so it does not wait on that.

It also respects Frank's 2026-08-26 rule ("ONE tag per row at most... our tags
are getting messy") — the chip slot already exists, no new tag is added.

### The stage error is a DIFFERENT AXIS — not a chip

It is not an outcome. It is a mismatch between what the call produced and what
the CRM says, it can co-occur with ANY outcome, and it is an action item rather
than a result. Two placements:

1. **Inline** — a small warning marker on the row, so it is seen in the context
   of the call that revealed it. In the coaching email this does not compete
   with the call back / call in badge for the left slot (that badge was on only
   1 of 19 rows tonight), and the coaching row has room for its own column.
2. **Aggregated** — a short "Pipeline Hygiene" block: "3 leads need stage moves"
   with lead links. This is what makes it actionable without hunting, and it is
   where PATTERNS surface: **Lorena has zero leads in FSD across the entire
   corpus**, which is only visible when they are listed together.

### NEEDS FRANK — the outcome -> expected stage map

Stage-error detection needs to know what stage each outcome implies. Only the
FSD case is unambiguous today: call says pending sale with a future effective
date, lead is not in 81248, therefore wrong. Everything else would be guesswork.

**Recommend shipping FSD-only detection first**, then extending the map as Frank
defines it.

## 14. MIGRATION: dashboard replaces the email  [STARTED 2026-09-01]

Frank: *"i want to start replacing the full email with this dashboard, lets begin
to do that."*

**The board:** a claude.ai artifact with the `db` capability. One document per
Arizona day in the `days` collection; the page renders whichever day is selected.
Private to Frank until shared; share read-only to give producers their own view.

### Why it replaces the email rather than supplementing it

- No 102,400-byte clip and no 16,384-byte stylesheet cap. Both were already
  binding: the coaching document hit 108KB before trimming.
- `<details>` collapses in a browser. In Gmail it does not, so the scorecards
  would ship permanently expanded in an email.
- History accumulates in one place instead of scattering across sent mail.

### Done tonight

- **`board_payload.py`** (new, in the repo). Builds the day document from
  `metrics_<day>.json`. Single definition of the shape so the nightly run and any
  backfill agree. Emits totals, producers, speed to dial, leaderboard, outcome
  breakdown, recontact, task audit, task completion.
- **Board at content parity** with the email's panels: headline tiles, per
  producer table, Team Leaderboard, Speed to Dial, Coaching & Call Quality, Task
  Completion, Recontact Struggle, Task Completion Audit, plus the Coaching tab
  (17 cards) and the cross-call findings the email never had.
- 2026-09-01 seeded and rendering.

### The board and the email now DISAGREE, deliberately

1. **Speed to Dial.** `board_payload.speed_to_dial` implements section 1: team
   quickest is the fastest single dial, team longest the slowest, team median the
   median of pooled times, and producers with no internet leads are dropped. The
   email still prints max-of-medians. **The board is right; the email is not.**
2. **Objection grades.** The board's coaching cards carry the section 13
   re-grades (Cecilia and Eva to solid red). The email carries the model's
   original grades. Until the prompt is corrected they will not match.

Both differences resolve when the patches land. Neither should be "fixed" by
making the board match the email.

### KNOWN APPROXIMATION — team median

`metrics_<day>.json` stores only per-producer summary stats, so the pooled
distribution is reconstructed from quickest/longest/median x n. **Quickest and
longest are exact; the median is approximate** -- 18s against a true 22s on
2026-09-01. Fixing it means `daily.py` storing the raw per-lead seconds, which is
part of the section 1 patch. Until then the board should not be quoted to the
second on team median.

### Remaining, in order

1. **Automate the write.** The nightly run must produce `out/board_<day>.json`
   (one call to `board_payload.build`) and the session hands it to the Artifact
   tool's `write_db`. NOTE: the artifact store has no HTTP endpoint we hold a key
   for, so a script cannot write it -- it is a Claude tool call. Keep
   `board_payload.py` pure; the session does the writing.
2. **Shrink the email.** Headline figures and a link, not nine panels. Keep it:
   people need the numbers on a phone and a nudge that the day is ready.
3. **Coaching payload into `board_payload.py`.** The 17 coaching cards are still

5. **Backfill.** Prior days can be written from their `metrics_<day>.json` with
