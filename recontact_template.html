How the Entered date is determined. Preferred source is the recorded stage move into that stage (“exact”). A lead is created into New and AgencyZoom writes no move note for that, so for early-funnel stages the lead’s creation date is the entry date (“from lead creation”). AgencyZoom overwrites enterStageDate when it moves a lead to Smart-Cycle, so that field reads as the outcome date and is never used here — trusting it collapsed every window to zero days and hid every call on the lead.

“Calls between” counts that producer’s dials to the lead’s number between entering the stage and the outcome, so a high count next to a loss is effort spent without a decision.
