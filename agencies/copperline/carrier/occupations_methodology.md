Northstar gives an occupational (affinity) discount to these groups -- the
carrier's list as published, each group with what it covers:

- **Educators**: teachers and professors, kindergarten through college
- **First responders**: firefighters, police and law enforcement, EMTs
- **Healthcare**: registered nurses, physicians, dentists, veterinarians
- **Engineers and scientists**: engineers, architects, scientists
- **Military**: active, veterans and retired
- **Retirees**: retirees of any eligible group still qualify
